How the numbers are built

  • Season is the water year, 1 July to 30 June.
  • River Zone 10 is NSW Murray above the Barmah Choke (upstream of Picnic Point) and River Zone 11 is below it. A register trade is placed by its NSW Murray side: the seller's zone if the seller holds NSW Murray water, otherwise the buyer's.
  • Water sold is register trades marked Standard Commercial and priced above $0, plus MIL Water Exchange sales. It leaves out related-party, environmental, carryover-parking, private-lease and forward-contract assignments. For trades with no recorded purpose, every trade above $0 counts. Supplementary water is excluded.
  • MIL Water Exchange trades happen inside Murray Irrigation's own licence and don't appear in the state register, so the two volumes add without double counting. That's derived from the data, not confirmed by MIL or WaterNSW.
  • Last 30 days is the 30 days to the latest trade date. The Choke spread is Zone 11's trailing 30-day average less Zone 10's (river trades), and less MIL's exchange price, which is also above the Choke.
  • Permanent entitlement sales are register share assignments (s71Q) and licence transfers (s71M) of NSW Murray General or High Security share components priced above $0. Price is dollars per unit of share component. The register records no trade purpose or zone for these, so related-party or token-priced dealings can only be caught by the outlier rule, which here leaves them out of volume as well as price: a price below × or above × the median for that category and season, once there are at least trades. Trades of Murray Irrigation's own irrigation rights happen inside its licence and aren't in the register.
  • Price is the volume-weighted average (VWAP), with the median beside it. Outliers still count in volume.
  • Waterfind publishes only its last ten settled trades per zone, so it's an indicator and never added to volume. Its Zone 11 list has matched other zones' lists and is flagged when that happens.
